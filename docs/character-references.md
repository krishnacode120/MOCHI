Olaf's revised snowball silhouette, twig hair/arms, carrot nose, coal buttons, tooth, and expressive grin retain the requested hugs, storytelling, summer curiosity, and detachable-body behavior. Mochi and Pip remain original designs.

Actions are scripted animation: no screen understanding, movie recognition, emotional inference, or character voice synthesis is performed. Webs and punches are decorative and never interact with other applications. Climbing uses monitor edges, not arbitrary application windows. Web swings stay within the available monitor work area.
